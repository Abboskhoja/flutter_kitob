Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

Flutter Kitob hisob (akkaunt) talab qilmaydi va shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni serverga yubormaydi. Ilova quyidagilarni faqat qurilmangizda saqlaydi:

  • siz kiritgan ism (ixtiyoriy);
  • o'quv progressi: tugatilgan darslar, XP, test natijalari, yutuqlar;
  • sozlamalar: mavzu, kunlik maqsad, eslatma vaqti;
  • Playground'da yozgan kodingiz.

Bildirishnomalar

O'qish eslatmalari qurilmaning o'zida rejalashtiriladi. Ular faqat siz ruxsat bersangiz yoqiladi va istalgan vaqtda ilova sozlamalaridan o'chiriladi.

Uchinchi tomonlar

Ilovada reklama, analitika yoki kuzatuv (tracking) vositalari yo'q. Ma'lumotlaringiz hech kimga sotilmaydi va berilmaydi. Darslardagi tashqi havolalar (masalan, dart.dev) brauzerda ochiladi va o'sha saytlarning siyosatiga bo'ysunadi.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Sozlamalar → "Barcha ma'lumotlarni o'chirish" progress, kodlar va sozlamalarni butunlay o'chiradi. Ilovani o'chirish ham ularni butunlay yo'q qiladi.

Bolalar

Ilova shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'magani uchun har qanday yoshdagi o'quvchiga xavfsiz.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

What we collect

Flutter Kitob does not require an account and does not send your personal data to any server. The app stores the following only on your device:

  • the name you enter (optional);
  • learning progress: completed lessons, XP, quiz results, achievements;
  • settings: theme, daily goal, reminder time;
  • code you write in the Playground.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are enabled only with your permission and can be turned off at any time in the app settings.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Your data is never sold or shared. External links in lessons (e.g. dart.dev) open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete all data" erases your progress, code and settings. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it is safe for learners of any age.
